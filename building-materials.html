Core Materials & Components

Review the role of each category, its common applications, and practical checks to discuss when specifying materials. Final selection should follow project design, applicable standards and qualified technical advice.

Strength & reinforcement

Steel

Steel provides tensile strength and structural support in buildings and civil works. In reinforced concrete, properly specified reinforcement works with concrete to resist loads and help control cracking.

Construction Steel & TMT Bars

TMT bars are widely used in RCC foundations, columns, beams, slabs and other designed elements. Construction steel may also be specified in reinforcement and fabricated components. Grade, bar diameter, ductility, test documentation and compatibility with structural drawings are key selection points.

Structural Steel

Beams, channels, angles and other sections support industrial sheds, long-span roofs, frames, platforms and infrastructure. Consider section dimensions, grade, fabrication quality, corrosion protection and connection details with the project engineer.

Selection check: confirm grade, dimensions, test certificates, storage condition and design specification before procurement.

Binding & durability

Cement

Cement binds concrete and mortar, making it essential to foundations, RCC work, masonry and plastering. The appropriate cement type and mix depend on the element, exposure conditions, specification and construction method.

Ambuja Cement & ACC Cement

We can discuss product availability from these recognized brands according to your requirement. Brand names are used for product identification; they do not imply an official dealership or authorization.

Before selection, check the intended application, applicable product standard, batch and manufacturing details, freshness, packaging condition and advice from the project’s qualified engineer.

Storage: keep bags sealed, dry and raised off the floor, protected from moisture; use older stock first and avoid hardened or damaged bags.

Reliable water systems

CPVC / PVC Pipes

Pipe systems carry water through homes, offices and commercial buildings. CPVC is commonly specified for hot and cold water distribution; PVC systems are used in suitable cold-water, drainage and related applications depending on product rating and local requirements.

Components for connected systems

  • CPVC pipes for appropriate hot- and cold-water lines.
  • PVC pipes for specified cold-water, drainage or service lines.
  • Plumbing pipes and fittings for joins, branches, valves and outlets.
  • Water supply pipes selected for pressure, temperature and use.

Confirm diameter, pressure and temperature rating, potable-water suitability where relevant, compatibility of pipe and fittings, jointing method and installation guidance.

Finish & protection

Paints

Interior and exterior coatings add colour and help protect surfaces when selected and applied to suit the substrate and exposure. Asian Paints and Berger Paints are included as brand options; availability and product suitability should be confirmed for each job.

Finish for the surface and space

Choose interior finishes based on room use, desired appearance and cleanability. Exterior coatings should suit weather exposure and the wall system. Decorative finishes can add texture or a feature effect when the underlying surface is prepared correctly.

Remove dust and loose material, repair defects, allow surfaces to dry and use compatible primer before applying the recommended coats. Finish type, sheen, coverage, ventilation and maintenance needs guide selection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which TMT bar should I use for my project?

Bar grade and diameter must match structural drawings and applicable standards. Ask the project’s structural engineer to specify the reinforcement; confirm markings and test documents at procurement.

How do I choose between CPVC and PVC pipes?

Choose based on the line’s temperature, pressure, fluid, application and local requirements. Check the product rating and ensure pipes, fittings and jointing materials are compatible.

How should cement be stored on site?

Store sealed bags in a dry, ventilated area, raised above the floor and protected from rain and damp walls. Follow manufacturer instructions and rotate stock so older bags are used first.

Are you an authorized Ambuja, ACC, Asian Paints or Berger dealer?

Brand names identify products customers may enquire about. This page does not claim official dealership or authorization. Ask us to confirm current availability and supply details.

Can you help estimate quantities?

Share project drawings or a bill of quantities for discussion. Final quantities should be calculated and approved by the project’s qualified designer or contractor.
