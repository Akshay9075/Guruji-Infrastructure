Understand the available record

Search Title

A property title search is a review of available ownership records and related documents to understand the recorded history and interests associated with a property. It helps buyers and their advisers identify questions that need further investigation before proceeding.

  • Gather and review available ownership and title documents.
  • Check relevant property records available from appropriate sources.
  • Note inconsistencies or missing information in the material reviewed.
  • Refer findings to a qualified professional for legal interpretation and advice.

A search is limited to records available and the scope of review; it does not guarantee a clear title or replace professional due diligence.

Clear terms in writing

Lease Deed

A lease deed records the agreed terms between a landlord and tenant. A written agreement helps both parties refer to the property and conditions they accepted during the lease period.

  • Landlord and tenant names and relevant details.
  • Accurate property description and permitted use.
  • Lease duration, rent, payment dates and security deposit.
  • Renewal, maintenance, notice and termination conditions.

We can help coordinate document preparation and registration assistance where applicable. A qualified professional should review terms for the parties’ needs and local requirements.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a property title search tell me?

It reviews ownership history and related documents available within a defined scope. Findings need interpretation by a qualified legal professional and do not by themselves guarantee title status.

Does a title search guarantee that a property is clear?

No. No title clearance or legal result is guaranteed here. A qualified professional should conduct and explain due diligence based on the property, records, scope and applicable law.

What should I check before buying property?

Arrange appropriate independent checks of ownership and title, encumbrances, land use, approvals, taxes, boundaries, physical condition and transaction terms. Requirements vary; seek qualified legal and technical advice.

Does every lease deed need registration?

Registration and stamping requirements depend on lease terms and applicable law. Consult a qualified local professional or the relevant registration authority to confirm what applies.

Can you provide legal advice?

We assist with coordination and documentation support. Legal advice, verification and opinions should be provided by appropriately qualified legal professionals.
